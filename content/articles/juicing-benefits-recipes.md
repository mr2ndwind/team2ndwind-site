## What Is Juicing?
Juicing extracts liquid from fruits and vegetables, providing a concentrated source of vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and phytonutrients.

It's an easy way to increase your daily produce intake and perfectly complements healthy eating habits.
- Fast nutrient absorption
- High concentration of vitamins
- Easy to digest
- Great way to consume raw vegetables

![Juicing process](assets/ce021160-2814-4622-80a6-817944cb4293.png)

## Benefits of Juicing
- **Increased nutrients:** helps increase vitamins and minerals from fruits and vegetables.
- **Hydration support:** many fruits and vegetables contain high water content.
- **Immune support:** vitamin C-rich ingredients may support healthy immune function.
- **Recovery support:** antioxidants help combat exercise-related oxidative stress.
- **Digestive health:** certain ingredients may support digestion and gut health.
- **Energy support:** natural carbohydrates and nutrients can support daily energy levels.

## Best Ingredients for Juicing
- **Greens:** spinach, kale, celery, cucumber
- **Fruits:** apple, pineapple, orange, lemon
- **Root veggies:** carrots, beets, ginger, turmeric
- **Recovery:** tart cherry, ginger, turmeric, citrus fruits

## Juicing vs Smoothies
- **Juicing:** faster digestion, lower fiber, concentrated nutrients
- **Smoothies:** higher fiber, more filling, great meal replacement option

Both can be part of a healthy lifestyle.

## Common Juicing Mistakes
Avoid:
- Too much fruit
- Added sugars
- Replacing all meals
- Ignoring protein intake
- Not eating whole foods

> COACH'S TIP | "Juicing can be a great way to increase your fruit and vegetable intake, but remember that consistency with nutrition, hydration, exercise, sleep, and recovery will always have the greatest impact on long-term health and performance." - Coach Corey, Team 2nd Wind
