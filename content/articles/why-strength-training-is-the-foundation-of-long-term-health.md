For decades, cardiovascular exercise was touted as the ultimate key to health. While cardio is important, strength training is the true foundation of longevity, mobility, and a high quality of life. At T2W Fit Studio in Broken Arrow, strength is at the core of everything we do.

## 1. Muscle Mass Prevents Injury
Strong muscles protect your joints. By strengthening the muscles around your knees, hips, and shoulders, you create a natural brace that absorbs impact and reduces the risk of daily injuries.

## 2. It Boosts Your Metabolism
Muscle is active tissue. It requires calories to maintain. By adding lean muscle mass through our coach-led training programs, you increase your resting metabolic rate, making it easier to stay lean year-round.

## 3. Bone Density and Aging
As we age, we naturally lose bone density, leading to conditions like osteoporosis. Resistance training places stress on the bones, signaling the body to build them back denser and stronger. This is crucial for adults over 30.

## 4. Better Posture and Mobility
Sitting at a desk all day wreaks havoc on your posture. Exercises like deadlifts, rows, and squats strengthen your posterior chain (the muscles on the back of your body), pulling your shoulders back and improving your overall alignment.

## 5. Mental Toughness
There is a profound psychological benefit to lifting heavy things. It teaches resilience, patience, and grit. The confidence you build in the gym directly translates to how you handle stress in the real world.
