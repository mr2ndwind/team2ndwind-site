Athletes spend countless hours training, but many overlook one of the most important performance factors: nutrition.

Proper fueling can improve:
- Energy levels
- Recovery
- Strength
- Speed
- Focus
- Overall performance

## Start With Protein
Protein helps repair and build muscle tissue. Great protein sources include:
- Chicken
- Turkey
- Lean beef
- Eggs
- Greek yogurt
- Fish
- Protein shakes

Athletes should aim to include protein with every meal.

## Carbohydrates Fuel Performance
Carbs are often misunderstood, but they are the body's preferred fuel source during high-intensity activity. Healthy carbohydrate options include:
- Rice
- Potatoes
- Oatmeal
- Fruit
- Whole grains

Athletes who avoid carbs often experience reduced energy during practices and games.

## Hydration Is Performance
Even mild dehydration can negatively impact performance. Athletes should:
- Drink water throughout the day
- Hydrate before workouts
- Replace fluids after training

Hydration should be a daily habit, not something athletes think about only on game day.

## Recovery Nutrition
The first hour after training is important. A recovery meal should include protein, carbohydrates, and fluids. Examples:
- Protein shake and banana
- Chicken and rice
- Greek yogurt with fruit

## Supplements Are Supplemental
Supplements can help fill nutritional gaps, but they should never replace healthy eating habits. Athletes should focus on sleep, nutrition, hydration, and training. Supplements come after those fundamentals are established.

At T2W Elite, we educate athletes on how to properly fuel their bodies for training, competition, and recovery.

Better nutrition leads to better performance, faster recovery, and long-term athletic success. Train hard. Recover smart. Fuel your body with purpose.
