Whether you're trying to increase your vertical jump, improve your first step, or become more effective on defense, explosive power training can help take your game to the next level.

## What Is Explosive Power?
Explosive power is the ability to produce force quickly. In basketball, explosive power impacts:
- Vertical jumping
- Sprint speed
- First-step quickness
- Rebounding ability
- Defensive reaction time
- Change of direction

Athletes who can generate force rapidly often gain a significant advantage over their competition.

## The Three Components of Basketball Power

### 1. Strength Development
Before athletes can become explosive, they must build strength. Key exercises include:
- Squats
- Split squats
- Romanian deadlifts
- Trap bar deadlifts
- Push presses

Strength creates the foundation for power production.

### 2. Plyometric Training
Plyometrics teach the body to apply force quickly. Examples:
- Box jumps
- Broad jumps
- Lateral bounds
- Depth jumps
- Skater hops

Quality and effort matter more than volume.

### 3. Speed & Agility Training
Athletes must learn to transfer power into sport-specific movement. Examples:
- Ladder drills
- Cone drills
- Sled sprints
- Reaction drills
- Change-of-direction training

## Recovery Matters
Explosive training places high demands on the nervous system. Athletes should prioritize:
- Sleep
- Hydration
- Proper nutrition
- Mobility work
- Recovery days
