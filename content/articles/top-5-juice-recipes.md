## 1. Green Energy Juice

![Green Energy Juice](assets/3146fd09-219e-4208-91ab-ea428819de59.png)

### Ingredients
- 2 medium cucumbers
- 2 green apples
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1 lemon, peeled
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger
- Optional: 1 stalk celery

Yield: approximately 16-20 oz.

### Benefits
- Hydration support
- Vitamin C
- Antioxidants
- Natural energy boost

### Instructions
Wash all ingredients thoroughly. Run through a juicer. Serve immediately over ice.

## 2. Recovery Red Juice

![Recovery Red Juice](assets/25003e4f-1ac4-4226-a078-d647258cd4c6.png)

### Ingredients
- 2 medium beets
- 4 medium carrots
- 1 apple
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger
- 1/2 lemon, peeled

Yield: approximately 16 oz.

### Benefits
- Supports exercise recovery
- Rich in antioxidants
- Natural nitrates may support circulation
- Excellent post-workout option

### Instructions
Wash and prep ingredients. Juice all ingredients and enjoy fresh.

## 3. Immune Boost Juice

![Immune Boost Juice](assets/47300896-6a8d-4c47-83d7-7debc5c8ce04.png)

### Ingredients
- 2 oranges, peeled
- 1 lemon, peeled
- 2 cups fresh pineapple chunks
- 1-inch piece fresh turmeric root
- 1/2-inch piece ginger

Yield: approximately 16 oz.

### Benefits
- Vitamin C support
- Antioxidants
- Anti-inflammatory compounds
- Great during cold and flu season

### Instructions
Juice all ingredients and stir before serving.

## 4. Morning Hydration Juice

![Morning Hydration Juice](assets/4368563f-fcf7-4434-b817-e02b45ba961f.png)

### Ingredients
- 1 large cucumber
- 4 celery stalks
- 1 green apple
- 1 lemon, peeled
- 4 fresh mint leaves

Yield: approximately 16 oz.

### Benefits
- Hydration support
- Refreshing flavor
- Low-calorie option
- Great first thing in the morning

### Instructions
Juice all ingredients and serve chilled.

## 5. Tropical Wellness Juice

![Tropical Wellness Juice](assets/0f973dc0-c1df-4276-9af5-e1b2a94e1b00.png)

### Ingredients
- 2 cups pineapple chunks
- 2 oranges, peeled
- 1 mango, peeled and pitted
- 1-inch piece ginger
- Juice of 1 lime

Yield: approximately 18-20 oz.

### Benefits
- Rich in vitamin C
- Digestive support
- Hydration
- Naturally sweet without added sugar

### Instructions
Juice pineapple, oranges, and ginger. Blend mango separately if needed and combine. Add fresh lime juice before serving.

## Coach Corey's Juicing Tips
- Aim for a 70/30 ratio of vegetables to fruit.
- Drink juice within 15-30 minutes for maximum freshness.
- Pair juice with a protein source for a more balanced snack.
- Use juicing to supplement healthy eating, not replace meals.
- Rotate ingredients weekly to maximize nutrient variety.

## Beginner Formula
Choose:
- 1 leafy green
- 1 cucumber or celery base
- 1 fruit
- 1 citrus
- 1 recovery booster (ginger or turmeric)

> EXAMPLE | Spinach + Cucumber + Apple + Lemon + Ginger
