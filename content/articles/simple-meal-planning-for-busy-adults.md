Between work, family, and training at T2W Fit Studio, finding time to eat healthy can feel impossible. But meal planning doesn't have to mean spending your entire Sunday in the kitchen. Here's how busy adults in Broken Arrow and Tulsa can simplify their nutrition.

## 1. Keep Your Menu Small
One of the biggest mistakes people make when meal planning is trying to cook a different recipe every night. This leads to massive grocery bills and hours spent cooking. Instead, pick 2-3 breakfast options, 2 lunches, and 3 dinners. Rotate them throughout the week.

## 2. Batch Cook Your Proteins
Protein is the foundation of recovery, especially if you're taking our coach-led training classes. Cook a large batch of chicken, lean beef, or turkey at the start of the week. You can easily add this pre-cooked protein to salads, wraps, or bowls when you're short on time.

## 3. Use the T2W Fit Training App
If you're a member of T2W Fit Studio, the T2W Fit Training App isn't just for workouts. You can use it to track your daily nutrition, ensuring you're getting the right balance of macronutrients to support your goals.

## 4. Embrace Frozen Vegetables
Frozen vegetables are just as nutritious as fresh ones, and they won't go bad if you forget about them. Keep your freezer stocked with broccoli, spinach, and mixed veggies. They can be steamed in minutes and added to any meal.

## 5. Plan for "Lazy" Meals
There will be days when things don't go according to plan. Have a backup option ready. A protein shake, a rotisserie chicken from the store, or eggs and toast can save you from hitting the drive-thru.
