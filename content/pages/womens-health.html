InBody for Women

Ditch the Scale

Track the metrics that actually matter for your health, hormones, and body composition.

  • Body fat %: your true body composition beyond total weight
  • Muscle mass: the lean tissue that drives metabolism and strength
  • Hydration: water balance, which impacts recovery and energy
  • Visceral fat: the internal fat that affects long-term health
  • Progress trends: the real results of your consistency over time
