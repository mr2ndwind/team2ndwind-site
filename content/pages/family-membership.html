Age Guidelines

Who Can Train

Ages 8-11

Eligible for Athletic Performance (SPA), Basketball Skills, Private Training, and Buddy Training.

Not eligible for general adult fitness classes.

Ages 12-14

Eligible after a Fitness Assessment, Coach Orientation, Parent Waiver, and Coach Approval.

Open Gym requires parent or coach supervision.

Ages 15-17

Eligible after assessment and approval.

May participate in group classes and approved Open Gym after demonstrating proper movement competency.

18+

Standard memberships apply.
