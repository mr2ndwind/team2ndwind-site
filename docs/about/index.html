About Team 2nd Wind

The premier fitness and athletic performance studio in Broken Arrow, OK. Built on hard work, discipline, and the belief that everyone has a second wind.

Founder & Head Coach

Meet Coach Corey

I didn't start T2W Fit Studio just to run people through workouts. I started it because I saw a gap in the fitness industry. People were either getting lost in massive boot camps with zero personalized coaching, or paying exorbitant fees for personal training they couldn't sustain.

As a former athlete, I missed the environment of a team: the accountability, the shared suffering, the collective push to get better. That's what we've built here.

Your "Second Wind" (T2W) is that moment when you think you have nothing left, but you dig deep and find another gear. We train you to find that gear, not just in the gym, but in life.
