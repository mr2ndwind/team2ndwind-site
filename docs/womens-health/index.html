InBody for Women

Ditch the Scale

Track the metrics that actually matter for your health, hormones, and body composition.

  • Body fat %: your true body composition beyond total weight
  • Muscle mass: the lean tissue that drives metabolism and strength
  • Hydration: water balance, which impacts recovery and energy
  • Visceral fat: the internal fat that affects long-term health
  • Progress trends: the real results of your consistency over time

Frequently Asked Questions
Is strength training safe for beginners?

Absolutely. Our programs are coach-led, meaning we teach you proper form, modify movements to your ability, and progress you safely as you build confidence.

How often should women complete InBody scans?

We recommend scanning every 4-6 weeks to track your muscle mass, body fat percentage, and hydration levels accurately without obsessing over the daily scale.

Can strength training improve metabolism?

Yes! Building lean muscle mass increases your basal metabolic rate (BMR), meaning your body burns more calories at rest, making long-term fat loss more sustainable.

What wellness stack is best for me?

It depends on your goals. Women's Performance is great for active individuals, while Hormone Optimize or Sleep & Stress can help with recovery and balance. Take our Wellness Assessment for a personalized recommendation.

Which T2W program should I start with?

Team Build or Personal Training are excellent starting points for establishing a safe baseline, learning proper mechanics, and progressively building strength.
