Wellhub & ClassPass

Wellhub & ClassPass Members Welcome

Train with us through Wellhub or ClassPass and experience what makes T2W different.

T2W on WellhubT2W on ClassPass

At Team 2nd Wind, we help busy adults and athletes build strength, improve conditioning, stay consistent, and find their 2nd Wind. Whether you are coming through your employee wellness benefit or trying us through ClassPass, we are ready to help you get a great workout and feel comfortable from day one.

What You Can Book

Wellhub and ClassPass members may be able to book eligible T2W classes based on availability inside their app. Available options may include:

  • Team Training
  • Strength Training
  • Conditioning Workouts
  • Core & Cardio
  • Open Gym options when available
  • Athlete Performance sessions when listed

Before Your First Visit

Book through your Wellhub or ClassPass app first.

Arrive 5-10 minutes early so we can welcome you, show you around, and help you get set up.

Bring water, comfortable training shoes, and be ready to work.

Want the Full T2W Experience?

Wellhub and ClassPass are a great way to try us out. If you are ready for more consistency, coaching, tracking, InBody scans, personal training, or unlimited access, we can help you choose the best T2W membership.
