Frequently Asked Questions
When should athletes start strength training?

Athletes can begin structured, age-appropriate strength training as early as 8-10 years old, focusing on bodyweight control, mobility, and movement mechanics before adding external loads.

Do athletes need supplements?

While whole foods should always come first, supplements like protein, hydration formulas, and recovery stacks can help fill nutritional gaps during intense training and competition seasons.

How often should athletes complete InBody scans?

We recommend athletes complete an InBody scan every 4-8 weeks depending on their training phase (off-season vs. in-season) to monitor muscle retention and hydration.

How is T2W Elite different?

T2W Elite focuses on competitive basketball team development, exposure, and advanced skill application in game scenarios, rather than just individual skill work.

How is SPA different?

Speed, Agility & Performance (SPA) is sport-agnostic. It focuses purely on making athletes faster, more explosive, and more agile regardless of the sport they play.
