Pause Policy FAQ

How long can I pause my membership?

You can pause for up to 3 months. Pauses are available for Studio memberships. App-only plans are not eligible for a pause.

Do I keep my membership rate when I resume?

Yes. A pause keeps your current membership price, including any grandfathered rate. When your pause ends, your membership resumes at the same price you started at.

Will I be billed while my membership is paused?

A $10 hold fee is charged for each month of the pause. This is what keeps your current membership price locked in. Your regular membership charge is suspended during the pause.

Can I access classes or Open Gym during a pause?

Membership benefits, including class access, Open Gym, and the T2W Fit App, are suspended while your membership is on hold. You'll regain full access the day your membership resumes.

What if I want to resume earlier than planned?

No problem. Just let us know and we can end your pause early so you can get back to training sooner. Reach out through the form above or contact us directly.

Is there a fee to pause my membership?

Yes. The pause fee is $10 per month of the pause. Pause requests must be submitted at least 30 days before your next subscription charge; requests submitted inside that window take effect after your next billing date.
