Frequently Asked Questions
What is wellness coaching?

Wellness coaching looks beyond just your workouts. We evaluate your sleep, stress, hydration, nutrition, and recovery to create a comprehensive plan for better health.

Do I need an InBody scan?

We highly recommend it. It gives us the data we need to personalize your approach and track your real progress over time.

Which stack is right for me?

Take the Wellness Assessment or speak with one of our coaches to get a personalized recommendation.

How often should I retest?

We recommend an InBody scan every 4-6 weeks to track your progress and adjust your wellness plan as needed.

Can beginners participate?

Absolutely. Our wellness ecosystem is designed to meet you where you are, whether you're just starting out or looking to optimize your current routine.
