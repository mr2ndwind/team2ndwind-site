Frequently Asked Questions
Can beginners succeed?

Absolutely. Our programs are designed to meet you where you are. We offer modifications and progressions to ensure everyone can succeed safely.

How long before I see results?

While everyone is different, most members start feeling better within 2 weeks. Measurable changes in strength and body composition typically appear within 4-6 weeks of consistent training.

How often should I do an InBody scan?

We recommend an InBody scan every 4-6 weeks to track your progress and adjust your training and nutrition as needed.

Which program should I start with?

Team Build is our recommended starting point for most new members, focusing on foundational movement patterns and building work capacity.

Can I switch programs?

Yes! Our programs are designed as a progression. As you build strength and capacity, you can transition from Build to Sweat to Flex.
