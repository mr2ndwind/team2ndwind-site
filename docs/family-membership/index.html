Age Guidelines

Who Can Train

Ages 8-11

Eligible for Athletic Performance (SPA), Basketball Skills, Private Training, and Buddy Training.

Not eligible for general adult fitness classes.

Ages 12-14

Eligible after a Fitness Assessment, Coach Orientation, Parent Waiver, and Coach Approval.

Open Gym requires parent or coach supervision.

Ages 15-17

Eligible after assessment and approval.

May participate in group classes and approved Open Gym after demonstrating proper movement competency.

18+

Standard memberships apply.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can my child join regular classes?

Children ages 12-17 may participate after completing an assessment and receiving coach approval.

Does a parent have to be a member?

Yes. Family Add-Ons require an active parent membership.

Can my child use Open Gym?

Only with coach approval or parent supervision.

What if my child is an athlete?

Young athletes can also train through our Speed, Agility & Performance (SPA), Basketball Skills, private, and buddy training options.
