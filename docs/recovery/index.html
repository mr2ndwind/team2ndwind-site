Frequently Asked Questions
How much sleep do I need?

Most adults need 7-9 hours of quality sleep per night for optimal recovery, hormone regulation, and cognitive function. Athletes may need even more.

How much water should I drink?

A general baseline is half your body weight in ounces per day, plus additional hydration to replace fluids lost during exercise.

Can recovery improve fat loss?

Absolutely. Poor recovery elevates cortisol, which can increase fat storage and cravings. Proper sleep and stress management are essential for sustainable fat loss.

How often should I retest InBody?

We recommend scanning every 4-6 weeks to accurately track your progress and ensure your recovery protocols are working.

What stack is best for recovery?

The Recovery & Joint stack is ideal for physical recovery, while the Sleep & Stress stack is best if you struggle to wind down or get quality rest.
