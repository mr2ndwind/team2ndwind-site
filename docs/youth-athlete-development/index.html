Frequently Asked Questions
What age should my athlete start training?

Athletes can begin foundational movement and coordination training as early as 8-10 years old. We focus on bodyweight control and mechanics before adding resistance.

Do young athletes need strength training?

Yes, when done properly. Age-appropriate strength training is safe and highly effective for injury prevention, building bone density, and improving overall athleticism.

How often should athletes train?

For optimal development without overtraining, we typically recommend 2-3 sessions per week, depending on their current sports season and schedule.

What sports benefit from this training?

All sports! We build general athleticism (speed, strength, coordination, and power) which transfers to basketball, football, soccer, baseball, volleyball, and more.

How often should athletes complete InBody scans?

We recommend athletes complete an InBody scan every 4-8 weeks depending on their training phase (off-season vs. in-season) to monitor muscle retention and hydration.
