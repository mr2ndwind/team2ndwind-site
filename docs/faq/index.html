FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about T2W Fit Studio, Team 2nd Wind training programs, open gym access, personal training, athletic performance, and digital coaching.

General

What is T2W Fit Studio?

T2W Fit Studio is a coaching-based fitness and performance training facility in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma that helps adults and athletes improve strength, conditioning, mobility, performance, and overall health through structured programs and expert coaching.

What services do you offer?

We offer Team Training Classes, Personal Training, Buddy Training, Speed, Agility & Performance (SPA) Training, Basketball Skills Training, T2W Elite Basketball Development, Nutrition Coaching, InBody Scans, Open Gym Access, and the T2W Fit Training App.

Do I need to be in shape before joining?

No. Our programs are designed for all fitness levels. Whether you're just getting started or looking to take your performance to the next level, our coaches can help.

What should I bring to my first workout?

Bring comfortable workout clothes, athletic shoes, a water bottle, and a positive attitude. We'll guide you through the rest.

How do I get started?

The easiest way to get started is by completing our contact form or signing up for a free trial. A coach will reach out to discuss your goals and recommend the best program for you.

Memberships

Do you offer a free trial?

Select programs may include a free trial or introductory offer. Contact us for current promotions and availability.

Personal Training

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. We offer one-on-one and buddy training sessions customized to your goals, fitness level, and schedule.

Open Gym

What is Open Gym?

Open Gym allows approved members and pass holders to train independently during designated Open Gym hours. Open Gym is intended for self-directed workouts and does not include coaching or instruction.

How does Open Gym access work?

Members must reserve Open Gym sessions through the T2W Fit app or booking system. Access may include digital entry credentials, temporary access codes, or staff-assisted entry depending on membership type and facility policies.

Are there rules for Open Gym?

Yes. All participants must check in before training, follow posted facility rules, re-rack equipment after use, respect other members, avoid tailgating or allowing unauthorized guests inside, and follow all safety guidelines. Failure to follow Open Gym policies may result in loss of access privileges.

Athletic Performance

Do you offer programs for athletes?

Absolutely. Our Speed, Agility & Performance (SPA) and T2W Elite programs help athletes improve speed, power, strength, agility, conditioning, and sport-specific performance.

T2W Fit Training App

What is the T2W Fit Training App?

The T2W Fit Training App provides access to workouts, coaching, progress tracking, nutrition support, accountability tools, and on-demand training from anywhere.

InBody

Is my first scan free?

Yes! We offer a complimentary first InBody scan to help you establish your baseline and set clear, measurable goals.

How long does an InBody scan take?

The scan itself takes less than 60 seconds. The entire appointment, including reviewing your results with a coach, takes about 15-20 minutes.

How often should I scan?

We recommend scanning every 4-6 weeks to accurately track your progress and make necessary adjustments to your program.
