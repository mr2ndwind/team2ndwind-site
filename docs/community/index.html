Find Your 2nd Wind

Community Is Part of the Workout

T2W Community brings our members, families, friends, athletes, coaches, and supporters together outside the studio through fun, affordable, active experiences throughout Broken Arrow and the Tulsa area. Most community events are free or very low cost, family-friendly when appropriate, and open to members bringing a friend. Being healthy is not limited to being inside a gym.

Get Out. Get Moving. Get Connected.

From sunrise walks and Turkey Mountain hikes to coffee meetups and seasonal events, we're building a community that goes beyond the walls of the studio.

What We Do Together

Community Events

Current dates, times, and meeting spots are posted on our schedule and in the T2W Fit Studio app. Registration is handled securely through T2W's PushPress system.

FreeBeginner Friendly

Morning Community Walks

Easy-paced social walks at LaFortune Park and Downtown Broken Arrow, often timed before the 9:00 AM studio class. Members and non-members welcome.

FreeFamily Friendly

Turkey Mountain Hikes

Early-morning community hikes at Turkey Mountain. Manageable pace, with options to turn around early for the 9:00 AM class.

Members + Friends

Coffee + Walk

Get some steps in, then stick around for coffee and conversation with the T2W community.

FreeRecovery

Sunday Recovery Walks

No workout. No competition. Just movement, conversation, and community. All fitness levels welcome.

Family Friendly

Seasonal & Holiday Outings

Halloween in Downtown Broken Arrow, holiday lights meetups, gratitude walks, and New Year reset walks.
