Frequently Asked Questions
How often should I complete an InBody scan?

We recommend scanning every 4-6 weeks to track your muscle mass preservation, body fat percentage, and hydration levels accurately.

How do I join a challenge?

Ask a coach during your next studio visit or check the T2W Fit Training App for current member challenges.

How do referrals work?

Refer a friend who joins T2W Fit Studio or T2W Elite Basketball and you earn 100 Rewards Points. Submit your referral on our Refer & Earn page.

How do I access resources?

All free guides, including nutrition guides and cookbooks, are available in the Resource Library below.

What are my member benefits?

As a T2W member, you get access to exclusive challenges, partner discounts, early event registration, and our wellness tools.
