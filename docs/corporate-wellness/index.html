Frequently Asked Questions
What is corporate wellness?

Corporate wellness refers to programs and activities designed to support and improve the health, fitness, and overall well-being of employees within an organization.

Can you come to our office?

Yes! We offer on-site services including InBody events, Lunch & Learn sessions, and fitness workshops.

How many employees are required?

We work with organizations of all sizes, from small teams of 5 to large corporations with 50+ employees.

How long does an InBody event take?

A typical InBody scan takes less than a minute per person. We can efficiently process large groups while providing individual result reviews.

Can we customize wellness programs?

Absolutely. We tailor our challenges, workshops, and coaching to fit your team's specific goals and company culture.
