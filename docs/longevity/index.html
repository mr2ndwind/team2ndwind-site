Frequently Asked Questions
Why is muscle important after 40?

Muscle mass naturally declines with age (sarcopenia). Maintaining muscle supports your metabolism, protects your joints, improves balance, and is a key predictor of longevity and independence.

Can strength training improve longevity?

Yes. Consistent strength training improves bone density, regulates blood sugar, reduces injury risk, and enhances overall quality of life as you age.

How often should I complete an InBody scan?

We recommend scanning every 4-6 weeks to track your muscle mass preservation, body fat percentage, and hydration levels accurately.

What supplements support healthy aging?

Our Longevity 40+ stack and Foundational Health stack provide the essential nutrients, antioxidants, and cellular support needed for healthy aging.

Which T2W program is best for beginners over 40?

Team Build or Personal Training are excellent starting points. They focus on movement quality, establishing a safe baseline, and progressively building strength.
